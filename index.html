To My Dear Students

Every classroom eventually reaches its final lecture, and today, mine does too. Although I may no longer stand before the whiteboard, I sincerely hope the curiosity we shared never leaves you. Do not study Physics merely to pass examinations. Study it to understand why the stars shine, why rainbows appear, why the universe obeys beautiful mathematical laws. Never fear difficult problems. Every equation that seems impossible today will become simple after enough patience. Remember: Marks may open doors, but character decides how far you will walk. Be kind. Stay humble. Question everything. Read beyond the syllabus. Respect your parents. Help those who struggle. And never stop learning. If, years from now, you remember even one concept, one lesson, or one piece of encouragement from my classes, then I shall consider myself a successful teacher. I may be leaving this school, but I will always remain proud to have been your teacher. I wish every one of you success, peace, and a life filled with purpose. — Junaid Ul Ahsan

One Last Hint...

Life is much like solving a Physics problem. If the answer doesn't make sense, don't immediately change the answer. Go back and check your assumptions. The same is true for life. Whenever you feel lost, pause, rethink, learn, and begin again. The strongest people are not those who never fail, but those who never stop trying.
